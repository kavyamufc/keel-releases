Privacy

Keel collects nothing about you. No analytics, no tracking, no account, no ads.

Last updated 26 November 2026

What stays on your Mac

Everything Keel knows about your Mac stays on it: the list of your apps, the update History, backups of old versions, your settings, Keel's logs and its record of which servers it contacted. None of it is uploaded.

If you add a GitHub token, it's kept in the macOS Keychain and sent only to GitHub's API.

What Keel contacts

To find updates, Keel asks each app's own update source whether there's a newer version. It sends only what that question needs:

  • Apps' own update feeds (Sparkle and Electron feeds, Microsoft AutoUpdate): the feed address the app itself uses.
  • Apple's App Store lookup (itunes.apple.com): the app's identifier, such as com.example.app, and the two-letter region from your Mac's settings, so the right store answers.
  • GitHub (api.github.com): the name of the app's release repository.
  • Homebrew (formulae.brew.sh): Keel downloads Homebrew's public list of apps; nothing about your apps is sent.
  • Keel's own updates and app catalog (kavyamufc.github.io): a plain download, like opening a web page.
  • Downloads of the updates you choose to install, from wherever the app's developer publishes them.

Every request identifies itself only as "Keel/1.0 (Macintosh)". Like any connection on the internet, these servers see your IP address; Keel doesn't add anything else. You can see every server Keel contacted, and why, in Keel under Connections.

Only when you click

  • Send Feedback opens a GitHub issue in your browser with your Keel and macOS versions filled in. You see it before anything is sent, and nothing is sent unless you submit it.
  • Request Coverage asks for an app Keel can't check yet to be added. Today it opens a GitHub issue in your browser with that app's name, identifier and version; nothing is sent unless you submit it.
  • Buy Me a Coffee opens buymeacoffee.com in your browser. Any payment is handled by Buy Me a Coffee and Stripe under their own privacy policies; Keel never sees it.

This website

Keel's website and downloads are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep server logs, under GitHub's privacy statement. The site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers of its own.

Questions
