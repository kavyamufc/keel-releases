• An app whose installer renames it is no longer reported as failed: after an update, Keel finds the app again by its identifier, counts the update when the new version is there, and says so ("Updated to 158.0.4370.33 · now Copilot.app"). Keel's list, reopening and later updates use the new name. This happened with Microsoft 365 Copilot, which Microsoft renamed to Copilot.
